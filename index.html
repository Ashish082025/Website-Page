05FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly do you build?+

A full email system: eight core flows including your pop-up. Welcome through winback, each one built to hold up over time, not just launch week.

Who is this actually for?+

Shopify brands run by their founders, where flows are missing, half-finished, or overdue for a rebuild. If that's you, the call is worth the half hour.

What tools do you use?+

Every email starts as a design file, the way an agency would build a print campaign — never a stock template. It's then built to run in Klaviyo, where your sends actually go out. On something else? Tell us on the call.

How long until it's live?+

The full system ships inside 90 days, guaranteed. Flows go live one at a time as they pass review, so early ones are already earning before the last one ships. Miss the date, and we keep working free until everything's live, plus your final payment back.

What's the cost?+

One number, quoted after we've seen your store on the call. No hourly rate, no line items, no surprises later.

We already have flows running. Worth a call anyway?+

Usually, yes. Existing flows tend to hide the cheapest wins. We'll walk through what we'd change and why, free.

Do you handle campaigns too?+

The studio's focus stays on flows — built once, then earning quietly in the background. Once yours are dialed in, campaigns are a natural next conversation.

What do you need from us?+

Not much: one kickoff call, access to Shopify and Klaviyo, and a yes or no on each flow before it ships. Everything else is on us.
